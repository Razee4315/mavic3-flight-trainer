RC-N1 controls and stick modes

How the DJI RC-N1 sticks work, and how they map to your keyboard or gamepad.

Stick modes

Pick the same mode your real remote uses (DJI Fly → Control → Stick Mode). Mode 2 is the default.

ModeLeft stickRight stick
Mode 2Throttle ↕ · Yaw ↔Pitch ↕ · Roll ↔
Mode 1Pitch ↕ · Yaw ↔Throttle ↕ · Roll ↔
Mode 3Pitch ↕ · Roll ↔Throttle ↕ · Yaw ↔

Hold Shift for gentle stick input. You can also drag the sticks on the 3D remote with the mouse.

Starting the motors

DJI drones use the Combination Stick Command: pull both sticks down and inward until the motors spin, then push the throttle up gently. After landing, hold the throttle down until the motors stop.
