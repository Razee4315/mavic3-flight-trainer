Flies like the real thing

Built around the Mavic 3's published specs and behaviour.

Cine, Normal, Sport

5, 15 and 21 m/s top speeds, just like the real flight mode switch.

GPS hold and braking

Let go of the sticks and it stops and hovers. Lose GPS and it drifts.

Obstacle sensing

Braking in Normal and Cine. Off in Sport, just like the aircraft.

Return to Home

Climbs, flies home and lands. Smart low-battery RTH included.

Gimbal and zoom

Tilt from −90° to +35°, zoom up to 28x, take photos and video.

A 3D RC-N1 remote

How it works

  1. Pick your stick mode

    Match your real remote. Mode 2 is the DJI default.

  2. Follow the lessons

    From the first takeoff to Return to Home, one skill at a time.

  3. Fly free

    Practise in wind, on a low battery, or in Sport mode.
